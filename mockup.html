الباب الأول: مفهوم مقاصد الشريعة الإسلامية

الشريعة الإسلامية نظامٌ ربانيٌّ شاملٌ، أنزله الله تعالى لتنظيم جميع شؤون الحياة الإنسانية، وقد بُنيت على غاياتٍ نبيلةٍ تُعرف بـ «المقاصد» الرامية إلى جلب المصالح ودرء المفاسد.


وقد اعتنى علماء الفقه والأصول بدراسة هذه المقاصد عنايةً بالغة، إذ هي الأساس الذي تُبنى عليه الأحكام الشرعية، والمرجعُ في فهم الحكمة الإلهية وراء الأحكام.


وكان الإمامُ الشاطبي رحمه الله من أبرز العلماء الذين نظّموا نظرية المقاصد وأسّسوا لها في كتابه الجليل «الموافقات» الذي ظلّ مرجعاً لا يُجارى في هذا العلم.


وتتمثّل الكليات الشرعية في «الضرورات الخمس»: حفظ الدين، والنفس، والعقل، والنسل، والمال.

🇬🇧 English
Chapter One: The Concept of the Maqasid of Islamic Sharia

Islamic Sharia is a comprehensive divine system, revealed by Allah Almighty to organize all aspects of human life. It is built upon noble objectives—called the "Maqasid"—that aim to bring benefit and ward off harm.


The scholars of jurisprudence (Fiqh) and the fundamentals of law (Usul) have paid significant attention to studying these objectives, as they are the foundation upon which legal rulings are built and serve as a reference for understanding the divine wisdom (Hikmah) behind the injunctions.


Imam Al-Shatibi (may Allah have mercy on him) was among the most prominent scholars to organize and elaborate upon the theory of the Maqasid, in his great work "Al-Muwafaqat," which remains an unmatched reference in this science until today.


The comprehensive objectives (Kulliyyat) of Sharia are known as the "Five Necessities" (Al-Daruriyyat al-Khams): the preservation of Religion, Life, Intellect, Lineage, and Wealth.

"And if you find them clinging to the covers of the Ka'bah," and he was known by the nickname Ikrimah bin Abi Jahl; because they used to believe in the old order that fought against the religion and oppressed the Muslims.
But his wife had sought the Prophet ﷺ as a believer, and came to him from Yemen. When he arrived to her, she greeted him warmly and said: "Welcome, the migrant rider." He said: What do you say, O Prophet of Allah? He said: Testify there is no god but Allah, and that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger. She said: Then what? He said: Say: O Allah, guide my emigrant fighter. So she did.
Then the Prophet ﷺ said: "I never give a questioner anything except I give you the same." He said: I do not ask You for wealth — Quraysh have plenty of it — but I ask that You forgive me.
Then he said: O Messenger of Allah, by Allah, I will not leave any position I used to stand blocking the path of Allah, except I stand in its like in the path of Allah — nor spend anything I used to spend blocking the path of Allah, except I spend its like in the path of Allah!
When the day of Yarmouk came, he dismounted and fought fiercely, and was struck a severe blow, and was found among seventy wounds — stabs, strikes and arrows.
It is reported that when Khalid bin al-Walid dismounted for him, he said: Do not — for that will kill him. He said: Leave me, O Khalid; he was with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ before you, and we were the harshest of people against the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. And so he remained until he was killed.
